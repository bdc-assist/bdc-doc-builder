# Workspaces

A workspace holds your data, notebooks and workflows in one place, and every analysis runs inside one.

## Creating a workspace

Choose a name, a billing project and an authorization domain. The authorization domain decides who can ever see the data in the workspace.

### Sharing a workspace

Add collaborators by email as reader, writer or owner. Collaborators must be members of the authorization domain to open the workspace.

## Billing projects

Compute and storage in a workspace are charged to its billing project. Ask your institution's billing administrator to add you to a project before you create a workspace.
