# Getting started

Welcome to the platform documentation. This page walks a new researcher through the first steps.

## Create an account

Sign in with your institutional login, then accept the data use agreement. Your account is ready within a few minutes.

## Uploading data

Use the upload wizard in your workspace to add your own files. Files up to 50 GB upload directly from the browser.

## Next steps

Read the PIC-SURE guide to search across studies, or open a workspace to start an analysis.
